# GRANTMARK architecture

GRANTMARK is a standalone StudioNet grant-milestone escrow. A sponsor locks one GEN tranche for one beneficiary and a precise, immutable rubric. The beneficiary supplies a short report and one or two public HTTPS evidence links by the submission deadline. The sponsor may lodge one objection with a public evidence link before the review deadline. After review, any account may request adjudication. GenLayer validators independently fetch the bounded pages and decide MET, NOT_MET, or INCONCLUSIVE under the locked rubric. MET releases the tranche to the beneficiary; NOT_MET or INCONCLUSIVE returns it to the sponsor. The sponsor cannot unilaterally reject evidence or change the rubric.

Funding uses a recoverable wallet credit. A payable deposit credits the sender; grant creation spends that credit and locks it. Settlement credits the winner, who withdraws in a separate pull-payment transaction. A missed submission expires to a sponsor refund. Adjudication must be initiated within seven days after the review deadline; after that anyone may invoke a timeout refund. These branches prevent indefinite locked funds.

The contract stores terms, timestamps, submitted evidence, objection, independent evidence snapshots, citations, decision, and settlement in on-chain state. The frontend reads finalized state directly from StudioNet. Public web pages are treated as untrusted data. Evidence is limited to three pages total, 48 KB per response, and 6,000 extracted characters per page. A conclusive verdict requires a citation to a readable source. Validators compare the outcome and the independently fetched evidence snapshots, not merely JSON shape.

This is a single-milestone MVP. Multi-tranche grants, private evidence, identity verification, and human appeals are outside its contract semantics. StudioNet is a test network; funds are test GEN. Web evidence can change or disappear before resolution, so a submission should use durable public sources and the on-chain decision retains source digests and excerpts.
